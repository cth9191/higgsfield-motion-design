## Project wordmark

`docs/images/wordmark-light.svg` and `wordmark-dark.svg` are original project artwork. The ribbon symbol and motion trails are independent of Higgsfield's branding. The wordmark uses outlined [Space Grotesk](https://github.com/floriankarsten/space-grotesk) lettering, by the Space Grotesk Project Authors, available under the [SIL Open Font License](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/spacegrotesk/OFL.txt). No font download is required to display the SVGs. The light/dark variants preserve contrast; reduced-motion preferences disable the moving trails.
